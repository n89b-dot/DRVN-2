      Buying

      We hold the money. You open the box.

      Every order goes through DRVN, with payments by Stripe and buyer protection built in. Nobody is paid until the part has arrived and you've had time to look at it.

      How buyer protection works

      1. First

        Find what fits

        Filter by your car. Every listing shows the car it came off, and its condition is recorded at sale.

      2. Then

        Pay into the hold

        Payments by Stripe. Your money sits with DRVN, not the seller. No account needed to buy.

      3. When it lands

        Open the box

        You have 30 days after delivery to report a problem: it never arrived, arrived damaged, isn't as described, or is the wrong item.

      4. Two days after delivery

        The seller is paid

        Unless you've reported a problem. Not a minute before.
