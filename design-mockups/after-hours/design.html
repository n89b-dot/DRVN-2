SC 07 / RULES
07

Do's & Don'ts

Do

  • Treat sodium as light — glow, edge, text, one filled CTA per view.
  • Cinematic statements in Clash Display; calm reading & slates in Satoshi.
  • Give every scene a film-slate in its corners.
  • Build depth from glow, haze, and a teal shadow floor.

Don't

  • Use sodium as a large flat fill beyond one CTA.
  • Lighten the night base past #0E1720 for content surfaces.
  • Round rectangular surfaces past 4px (pills & circles excepted).
  • Make motion quick or linear — scenes ease long at cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1).
