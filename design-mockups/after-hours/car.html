SC 02 / THE STORY
13B-REW

Nine years, told in three cuts

It arrived on a flatbed from Osaka with a dead apex seal and a glovebox full of Kansai toll receipts.

Every panel of the widebody went on in a rented railway arch, between midnight and four, because that's when the arch was quiet.

Final form means letting go. The parts coming off aren't spares — they're chapters.

THE RECORD

2017 · 03:20 AM · Felixstowe docks

Import night

Container opens. First UK start attempt fails — apex seal confirmed dead. Bought anyway. No regrets recorded.

2019 · 01:15 AM · Railway arch, E1

Rebuild #1 — 13B-REW out

Full engine-out rebuild. Ported, balanced, new seals throughout. First fire-up at 4am woke the whole arch row. Worth it.

Widebody + RE-Amemiya aero

Kit fitted, gapped, painted midnight grey with a warm pearl that only shows under sodium light. Dolphin tail exhaust and carbon wing go on the same month.

2026 · 01:47 AM · Level B2

Öhlins and a new titanium system going in. The KW V3s, the dolphin tail, and the carbon wing are released to the marketplace — with their full history attached.
