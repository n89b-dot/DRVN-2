How the record works.

Field method · 01–03
Specimen 01

Catalogue the vehicle

Log the build as a field entry — engine, suspension, tyres, coordinates and keeper. The car becomes the context every part is measured against.

Specimen 02

Log a part with provenance

Release a part from the vehicle and its history follows: which car, what it ran with, fitment basis, and the keeper's record — all stamped to the listing.

Specimen 03

Sell to verified fitment

Buyers match the specimen to their own platform. When fitment is platform-verified, not just keeper-stated, the sale carries the trust of a kept record.
