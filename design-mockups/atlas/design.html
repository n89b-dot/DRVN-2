07 · Guardrails

Do's & Don'ts

Do

  • Set coordinates, catalog numbers and dates in Spline Sans Mono, tabular.
  • Number specimens; caption with coordinates.
  • Mark the basis of any fitment claim — verified vs stated.
  • Olive for structure and links; clay almost only for prices.
  • Calm motion — 400–550ms ease-out, lines draw, cards rise 6px.

Don't

  • No pure white, cool grey, or bright saturated colour.
  • No regular grid or dimension lines — contours only.
  • No radii above 6px, no shadow heavier than the soft lift.
  • Don't spend clay on decoration.
  • Don't set body or headings in the mono voice.
