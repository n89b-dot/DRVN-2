Trust & safety

Terms of use

What DRVN is

DRVN is a place where people who build cars log what they've done and sell the parts that come off. We host the sheets, run the listings, hold the money while an order is in motion, and move it when the order completes.

What DRVN isn't

We are not the seller. We don't own, store, handle, inspect, test or ship any part listed here. The contract of sale is between the buyer and the seller; we're the venue and the escrow, nothing more. Where a part's description, fitment or provenance turns out to be wrong, that is the seller's statement, not ours — though we will hold the money and decide the dispute, which is a real remedy and the reason to stay on the platform.

Using an account

  • You must be 18 or over, and one person gets one account.
  • What you write on a sheet has to be true. Provenance is the whole product here — a fabricated build history is grounds for removal, not a warning.
  • You're responsible for what happens under your login. If you lose control of it, tell us and we'll freeze payouts within the hour.
  • We can remove a listing, pause selling or close an account for breaking these terms. We'll say which rule and give you one reply to appeal, answered inside 3 business days.

The 48-hour inspection window

Every paid order runs on the same clock. When the carrier marks a part delivered, the buyer has 48 hours to open the box and check it against the listing. During that window the money is held — the seller can see the sale, but can't spend it.

  • If the buyer does nothing, the window closes and the payout is released automatically. Silence means acceptance.
  • If the buyer opens a dispute before it closes, the money stays held until the dispute resolves — see Disputes.
  • Fitting the part ends the window early in practice. A fitted part can rarely be shown to have arrived faulty, and disputes on fitted parts usually fail.
  • Parts sold "for spares or repair" still get the window, but only for what the listing claimed — not for the fact that it doesn't work.

Privacy & data

Here is everything we hold, why we hold it, and how long. If a row below doesn't have a reason you'd accept, we shouldn't be storing it.

What we store

  • Account — name, email, phone. To run the account and to reach you about an order. Kept while the account exists.
  • Garage sheets — cars, mods, dates, photos. Public by default because provenance only works in public. You can set any sheet private; it then shows to no one but you.
  • Chassis and VIN — stored encrypted, never shown publicly, never given to a buyer. We use the last characters only to match fitment, and we release the full number to no one except on a valid legal request about a stolen vehicle or part. It doesn't appear on listings, on your keeper sheet, or in exports another person can see.
  • Addresses — shared only with the counterparty of a paid order, only after payment, and only until the order completes. Never shown on a profile.
  • Messages — kept 24 months. They're the evidence in a dispute, which is why they aren't deletable by either side once an order exists.
  • Payment data — held by our payment processor. DRVN never sees or stores a full card number. We keep the last four digits and the sale record.
  • Sale and payout records — kept 7 years, because tax authorities require it. This survives account deletion, in a minimised form.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal data, and we don't run advertising networks on the platform.
  • We don't put tracking pixels in transactional email. We do record whether the mail was delivered.
  • We don't share your location beyond the region you choose to display, which is a region, not an address.

Your rights

Export everything we hold as a file, from Settings, within 30 days of asking. Correct anything wrong. Delete the account — sheets, listings and messages go; the 7-year sale records stay, reduced to what tax law asks for. Under UK GDPR and the EU GDPR you can also complain to your national supervisory authority, and you don't have to go through us first.

Seller terms

Describing a part

  • Photograph the damage. A scored face, a kerbed lip, a weeping seal — show it. Sellers who photograph flaws almost never end up in disputes.
  • Say what the part came off. If you claim it came off a car, that car must be on your garage as a sheet with matching dates.
  • Fitment is your claim, not ours. "Fits a Delta HF Integrale" means you know it does, not that it probably does.
  • Ship within 3 business days of the sale, with tracking. Filing tracking advances the job card and starts the buyer's clock.

Prohibited items

Some things can't be sold here at all. This isn't caution — each one is either illegal to ship, dangerous when used, or impossible to make honest.

  • Airbags and pyrotechnic components — modules, inflators, seatbelt pretensioners. They are classed as explosives for shipping, and a mis-deployed unit maims people.
  • Counterfeit safety-critical parts — brake pads, discs, seats, harnesses, wheels, steering components branded as makers they aren't.
  • Stolen parts, or parts without clear title — including anything with ground-off or altered part numbers.
  • Cut VIN sections, plates and chassis tags.
  • Emissions-defeat hardware where it is illegal to supply — decat pipes and DPF deletes for road use in the UK and EU.
  • Tuned ECUs containing licensed software you don't have the right to pass on.

First removal pauses selling for 14 days. A second inside 12 months is 90 days. A third ends selling on the account. Buying, messages and your sheets stay available throughout.

One fee on a sale, plus what the card networks charge. Nothing to list, nothing monthly, no fee for a part that doesn't sell.

Worked example — a real one off this sheet

Momo Corse wheel set, sold for $310.00 to a buyer paying with a European card. Platform fee 5% — $15.50. Processing 1.4% + $0.20 — $4.54. Disputes, in plain English

Something arrives wrong. Here's the order things happen in, and what you can do if you don't like where it lands.

  1. Message the other person. Seven in ten disputes close here. The thread is already attached to the order.
  2. Open a dispute inside the 48-hour window. The money stops. The seller has 48 hours to respond; no response resolves it for the buyer automatically.
  3. We decide, if you can't. A person reads the listing, the thread and both sets of photos, and decides inside 3 business days. We can refund from the held funds — that's the whole point of holding them.
  4. If you disagree with us. You can take it to your local small-claims court, or to arbitration. Nothing in these terms removes the rights consumer law gives you, and UK and EU consumer rights sit above anything written here.

Arbitration is binding and individual — no class actions, said plainly rather than buried. You can opt out of the arbitration clause within 30 days of creating your account by emailing legal@drvn.cc with your username; opting out costs you nothing and changes nothing else. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.

We will never require you to arbitrate a claim about your own personal data, and we won't ask you to waive the right to complain to a data authority.
