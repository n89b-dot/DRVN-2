How offer windows work here

An offer holds for 48 hours. The seller can accept, counter once, or let it lapse — a counter restarts the clock on your side, and it only runs once. Withdrawing is free until the moment they accept. Sellers see how many offers you've honoured, so a lapsed offer costs you nothing but a withdrawn accepted one does.
