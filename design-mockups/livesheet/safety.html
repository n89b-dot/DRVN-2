VERIFICATION — WHAT THE BADGE MEANS Your keeper sheet
VERIFIED KEEPER  VERIFIED

Verified identity is not verified parts

The badge means one thing: a government ID was matched to the name on the payout account, and a phone number was confirmed. It says the person is real and reachable. It says nothing about whether a damper is worn, whether a wheel is straight, or whether a "genuine" part is genuine. Judge parts by the photos, the sheet they came off and the seller's history — not by the badge.

What we check ID, name, phone, payout Passport or driving licence matched to a selfie, name matched to the bank account money lands in, phone confirmed by code. DONE
What we don't check The parts themselves No one at DRVN handles, inspects or tests anything sold here. Fitment is the seller's claim; condition is what the photos show. NEVER
What it costs you Nothing, and it's private Your ID is handled by our identity provider and never stored on DRVN. Buyers see a badge and a first name — never a document, an address or a date of birth.
When it's required Above $2,500 lifetime Below that, selling works unverified. Above it, payouts hold until verification completes — anti-fraud rules we don't get to opt out of. THRESHOLD
What we store

About 4 minutes. ID photo, a selfie, and a phone code. Most checks come back inside 2 minutes; a manual review takes up to 1 business day.
