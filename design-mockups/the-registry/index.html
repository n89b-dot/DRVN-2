How the Registry works

Three procedures, borrowed from the auction house and the archive.

01

Register the car

Your car receives a registry number and a build ledger. Every modification is a dated entry — the paperwork your build always deserved.

02

Consign the part

When a part comes off, it leaves with papers: the ledger entry it was fitted under, mileage on the car, and what it ran alongside.

03

Trade on record

Buyers see the source car, the seller's dossier, and seller-stated fitment — clearly marked as such. The certificate transfers on sale.
