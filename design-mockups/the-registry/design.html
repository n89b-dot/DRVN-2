07

Do's and Don'ts

Do

  • Keep one accent — oxblood — for links, prices, and stamps.
  • Set headlines in Fraunces, coordinates in Archivo smallcaps.
  • Draw structure with the hairline rule and paper grain.
  • Give every listing a real certificate of provenance.

Don't

  • Round a card, button, or input past 0px (stamp is the exception).
  • Add a drop shadow — depth is rules, grain, and stamp only.
  • State seller fitment as fact; stamp it "not verified."
  • Let motion get quick or bouncy; reveals stay 400–700ms.
