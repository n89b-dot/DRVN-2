How the trace works

Three nodes. One continuous wire from car to buyer.

01 · Register

Register the car as a node

Your build becomes a schematic — platform, power, mods and every fitment fact, verified where the platform can confirm it.

02 · Release

Release a part — it inherits the wires

List a take-off and it stays wired to the car node: source, keeper rating, fitment basis and what it ran alongside travel with it.

03 · Trace

Buyers see the whole diagram

No detective work. The buyer reads the connection map and knows exactly what they are acquiring, and whether it fits.
