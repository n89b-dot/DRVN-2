"The Ludlow V8" — Aston Martin V8, 1977

No. I Coach-builder's specification
Engine 5.3-litre V8, converted from Bosch mechanical injection to four Weber 42 DCNF carburettors on a period Vantage-pattern manifold. Rebuilt 2023 by Hargreaves & Son · 328 bhp on the rolling road at Shrewsbury.
Suspension Koni adjustable dampers all round; front springs uprated one grade; original de Dion tube retained and re-bushed in polyurethane.
Brakes Ventilated front discs with four-piston callipers; braided lines throughout; original servo rebuilt rather than replaced.
Wheels & tyres 15″ GKN alloys (currently fitted) on Avon CR6ZZ; the chrome wire wheels it wore at purchase are released for sale below.
Interior Full retrim in Connolly-pattern tan hide with contrast saddle stitching; new Wilton carpet; original Smiths instruments superseded by a restored set. Retrim by Askew Trimming, Leominster, winter 2024.
No. II The build journal
Entry XIV · 6 July 2026

Carburettors, at last

Four Webers sit where the Bosch pump used to sulk, and the car has found a voice it never had on injection. Cold starts want two pumps of the throttle and a moment's patience; warm, it clears to an even idle at nine hundred. The old injection gear is crated, labelled, and will be entered into the ledger whole — someone restoring to factory will want it far more than I do.

Entry XIII · 18 May 2026

The de Dion question

Purists will tell you the de Dion tube is sacred; the bushes within it are not. Polyurethane all round, the tube itself blasted and painted in chassis black, and the rear of the car no longer wanders on the crown of a B-road. Alignment set at Bridgnorth with the fuel tank half full, as it should be.

Entry XII · 2 February 2026

On wire wheels, and letting go

Handsome as the chrome wires are, they belong to a gentler idea of this car than the one I am building. The GKN alloys save eleven pounds a corner and stiffen the steering's first inch. The wires come off with their hubs, their spinners, and their history — all recorded — and go to the ledger as a five-piece lot.
