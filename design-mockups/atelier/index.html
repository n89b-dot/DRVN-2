Provenance is the finest option a part can have.

DRVN is a marketplace kept like a ledger. Every part sold here is entered against the motorcar it came off, the hands that removed it, and the build it once served — so a buyer never purchases a rumour.

No. I The ledger principle
i.

The source motorcar

Each lot is bound to a documented car profile — chassis, specification, photographs, and the build journal in which the part appears. You see precisely what it came off, and why it was released.

ii.

The credible seller

Sellers are known by their garages, not their usernames. A seller's record — the cars they keep, the work they publish, the lots they have closed — travels with every listing they enter.

iii.

The documented pairing

Fitment is a matter of record here. Every part carries what it was paired with in service — the hubs, the arches, the linkage — so the question "will it fit" is answered before it is asked.
