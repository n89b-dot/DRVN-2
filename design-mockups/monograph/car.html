Provenance

The car came to Copenhagen in 2019 as a tired but honest Carrera 2 — original paint, a seized sunroof, and a folder of receipts going back to its first Stuttgart owner. Nothing about it was rare. Everything about it was intact.

The rebuild took the long road. The engine was opened not to chase power but to understand it; the ITB conversion followed only once the bottom end had been measured, shimmed, and trusted. The suspension was chosen to be lived with, not lapped. What you see is three winters of restraint.

When a part is released from this car, it leaves with the same folder it arrived in — dates, mileages, and the name of whoever last touched it. That is the whole point of keeping a car this way: so that the next person inherits certainty, not a story.
