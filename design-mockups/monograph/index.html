On provenance

We treat a build the way a gallery treats a photograph — hung in silence, captioned plainly, its origin never in doubt.

A modified car is an argument made in steel: a sequence of decisions, each one traceable to a maker and a reason. When a part leaves the car, that argument should travel with it. So every plate on DRVN answers the same four quiet questions — what car it came from, who kept it, whether it will fit yours, and what it once ran alongside.
