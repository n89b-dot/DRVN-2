# 19 · One Question

**Family:** interaction. The ritual is a single question and an answer that stays alive.
**Thesis:** The whole site is one question (what do you drive?) and everything after is the answer.

The front door is the question set at 11vw in Funnel Display, with one input underneath, written in the same family at light weight. Once you answer, the site re-forms around your car. Grids sort by fit and fade what doesn't fit. Headlines address the car ("Parts that fit your E46 M3"). The answer flies up into the nav and stays there as a chip you can change ("Your car: E46 M3 · change"), and changing it re-sorts every page live. "Just browsing" is always allowed: the car gives context and never blocks anything. The answer persists through localStorage (wrapped in try/catch) and a `?car=` parameter that every internal link carries.

The palette is one colour: the exact ultramarine of the product photography's paper sweep (`--field #0632B4`), and white. Products float on the field with radial masks, with no cards. Emphasis comes from scale, weight and inversion. Fit is shown by white intensity: a solid dot means it fits, a half dot means it fits with an adapter, an empty ring means it doesn't fit and the listing fades back. There is no second colour.

**Precedents:** Typeform's one-question-at-a-time flow, for the pacing of question and answer (web). Spotify Wrapped's personal reveal, for the moment the site turns to face *you* (web). Yves Klein's IKB monochromes, for the single saturated field as material (web). Lusion and Igloo Inc particle work, for points that form a solid body of light rather than a network (web).

**Showpiece:** a Three.js GPU particle car built from 48,000 points per car (RX-7 FD, E46 M3, EK9, 964). As you type, a fuzzy matcher resolves the car and the points travel along curl-field paths, staggered nose to tail, so the new car is drawn over about 1.4s. Interrupting a morph mid-flight continues smoothly because the JS mirror of the shader snapshots each point's exact position. An unknown car loosens into a hovering cloud. Submitting flares the car outward while the typed answer flies into the nav chip. Points are white additive soft sprites with ellipsoid-normal rim light, depth fade, a 5× glow pass, shimmer, a slow orbit and pointer parallax. Without WebGL it falls back to a 2D canvas projection of the same points. With prefers-reduced-motion, swaps are instant and nothing loops. DPR is capped at 2, and rendering pauses offscreen or when the tab is hidden. The garage reuses the renderer, and 2D projections of the clouds draw the tab and member thumbnails.

**Motion:** c19-motion-coilover.mp4 is used in part.html as the turntable hero for every coilover listing (muted, looping, inline, with the c19-coilover.jpg poster; paused under reduced motion).

**Responsive (390px):** the nav collapses to wordmark, compact car chip, cart and a Menu sheet. The question scales to 56px. On mobile the particle stage reframes into a band above the question (its own frame values, with the car centred at 90% width). Grids go from 3 or 4 columns to 2. Marketplace filters collapse into a disclosure. The checkout becomes a bottom sheet, and garage tabs scroll horizontally with the selected tab kept in view.

**Deviations, logged:** (1) A fifth page, sell.html, was added because Sell is in the nav and the one-question ritual fits listing a part ("What came off your E46 M3?" → price → review → listed). (2) The coilover video is actually 4:5, not 9:16, so the media frame is 4:5. (3) The wheel photo shows seven spokes, so the copy doesn't name a spoke count. (4) The white-background reference renders (c19-src-*) are not used. Particle silhouettes looked intentional on the field; the renders didn't. (5) The particle code is a third shared file (one-question-particles.js, an ES module).

**Flaw hunt (found → fixed):** the car read as glitter, not form → added rim light from an ellipsoid normal, depth contrast and a glow pass, and moved the camera to a truer 3/4 front (yaw 0.78, elevation 0.24). The answer underline crossed the car's nose → capped the answer line at 640px. A long ghost completion ("EK9 Civic Type R") clipped → auto-fit now measures the suggestion too. Product photos showed rectangle edges against the vignette → tighter radial masks and a fallback field-lift glow instead of a flat box. The submit flare blanketed the copy → halved the burst radius and size. The sell flow lost its gutters, and Back showed on step one → fixed padding and enforced `[hidden]`. The selected garage tab scrolled out of view on mobile → it now scrolls into view.

**Style-emulation prompt:** Build a single-colour site on a full-bleed ultramarine field (#0632B4, deepening to #022A9E at the edges, with a #0B45DE floor glow under objects) in white only. Translucent whites at 78%, 64% and 18% carry hierarchy; there is never a second colour. Set an enormous Funnel Display question (11vw, weight 600, −0.05em tracking, 0.86 leading) over a light-weight answer line with a thick custom caret and a 40%-white ghost completion. Let product cut-outs float on the field with radial masks: no cards, no borders, no shadows except a soft glow. Show state through white intensity and inversion: white pills with ultramarine text for primary and selected states, a solid, half or empty dot for status, and fading for "not for you". Make the hero a luminous particle form drawn in additive white points that re-forms as the user types. Keep copy short, warm and enthusiast-literate, addressed to the user's own thing.
